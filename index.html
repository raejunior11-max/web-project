The heart behind Nova

Made with love.
For every kind.

Nova was founded by Israel Ujah, a Nigerian pet lover with a soft spot for every paw, wing, hoof, and wild spirit. What began as a simple curiosity grew into a heartfelt vision: to help people slow down, look closer, and discover the rare qualities that make every animal wonderfully unique.

From the loyal warmth of a family dog to the quiet intelligence of a wild animal, Israel believes that every creature has something beautiful to teach us. Nova is his little corner of the world for sharing those stories, celebrating our fellow animals, and inspiring more kindness toward all living things.
